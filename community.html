I value the work of making knowledge approachable—whether that means exploring a river, building confidence in a classroom, or helping young people find their voice.

SUMMER
EDUCATION

Flint River Watershed Coalition

Education Intern

I led hands-on outdoor education programs, teaching children and community members how to collect water samples, identify local aquatic species, and assess river habitats. I also inventoried and organized environmental sampling kits and education gear to keep field days running smoothly. My goal was to make watershed science approachable and help younger generations learn how to care for local waterways.

PEER
EDUCATION

University of Michigan–Flint · BIO 113

Biology Peer Instructor

I facilitate weekly peer learning sessions and one-on-one tutoring, helping students break down complex biology concepts. I design custom practice exams, worksheets, and study guides to help students master difficult material and build confidence.

DUAL
ENROLLMENT

Genesee Early College

Social Studies Teaching Assistant

I support dual-enrollment students taking high school and college-level history and social studies courses. I create engaging classroom review games, formative assessments, and organizational systems that help students transition into the pace of college coursework.

STEM
MENTORSHIP

Team TESLA Robotics · FIRST Team 4998

Robotics Mentor

After six years with the team representing Genesee Early College at Kettering University’s FIRST Robotics Community Center, I now mentor students in engineering principles, machine shop operations, and team leadership. STEM education is a big part of my background, and helping students solve problems during build season is one of my favorite ways to support local youth.

CIVIC
ENGAGEMENT

Genesee County Board of Youth Commissioners

Youth Commissioner

Across two terms, I represented youth perspectives on county policy, community development, and civic engagement. In my second term, I joined the steering committee to help guide regional programming and give young people a direct voice in local governance. Our work focused on community revitalization, including anti-blight initiatives and improving local green spaces.
